Placeholders: {z} {x} {y}, {-y} (TMS), {q} (quadkey), {s} (subdomain).

Each source keeps its own saved tiles. Offline, the app falls back to any saved imagery for the spot. Google and Bing don't officially allow saving their tiles; they could stop serving them at any time.
